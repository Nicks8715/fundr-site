What Makes Us Different

We work for
your business.

Most businesses go straight to their bank — and spend weeks waiting for a decision. fundr takes a different approach. We know which lenders suit which industries, assets, and business profiles. That knowledge gets your deal across the line faster.

Independent — we work for you

fundr is not affiliated with any bank or lender. Our only job is finding the best deal for your business. We're paid when you get funded, so our interests are fully aligned with yours.

Fast — approvals in 24 hours

Most applications receive a decision within 24 hours. We structure your application correctly from the start, which means fewer delays and a smoother path to approval.

One person, start to finish

You deal with Nick — directly — from your first enquiry through to settlement. No call centres, no handoffs, no repeating yourself. Just one experienced broker who knows your deal.

15+ lenders, better rates

We present your deal to our full panel of NZ lenders — not just whoever is convenient. More competition means better rates, better terms, and more choice for your business.

Reliable & FSP registered

fundr is a registered Financial Services Provider (FSP1007390). You can trust that your application is handled professionally, ethically, and in your best interests at all times.

Lifetime support

Our relationship doesn't end at settlement. fundr provides ongoing admin and support for the life of your loan — and we'll be ready when you need finance again.
