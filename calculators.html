Questions

Are these calculators accurate?

They use standard loan maths with the inputs you give them, but rates shown are illustrative. Your real repayment depends on your credit profile, the asset and the lender.

Do the calculators affect my credit score?

No. They ask for no personal details and run no credit check.

Can I get a real quote?

Yes. Every calculator links to fundr's quote form, passing your amount and term across.
