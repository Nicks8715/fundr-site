Our Story

Why fundr
exists.

fundr was built on a simple observation: most Kiwi businesses were being underserved when it came to asset finance. The banks were slow, the process was opaque, and businesses were left waiting weeks for decisions on equipment they needed now.

Nick Sevta founded fundr to fix that. Having spent 15+ years inside institutions like BNZ, GE Capital, and Wells Fargo, he understood exactly why the system was broken — and how to navigate around it.

As an independent broker, fundr has no allegiance to any lender. We take your application to the right lenders for your deal, structure it to give it the best chance of approval, and get you a decision fast. Most within 24 hours.

The result is a business built on relationships, not transactions. Many of our clients have been with us for years — coming back every time they need a new vehicle, a piece of equipment, or a funding solution for their next step.

Your Broker

Meet Nick
Sevta.

Nick Sevta is the Managing Director of fundr and your direct point of contact for every deal. He founded fundr with one goal: to give Kiwi businesses access to the same quality of finance advice that was previously only available to large corporates.

With 15+ years in commercial lending across New Zealand and Australia, Nick has worked inside some of the biggest names in the industry. That experience means he knows exactly what lenders look for — and how to position your application to get the best outcome.

Nick's approach is direct and honest. He'll tell you clearly what's possible, what the options are, and what makes the most sense for your business. No jargon, no spin.

What We Stand For

Three things fundr
never compromises on.

These aren't marketing words. They're the principles that shape every conversation, every application, and every deal we work on.

Relationships over transactions

We're in it for the long term. fundr builds lasting relationships with clients — not just one-off deals. Most of our business comes from people who've worked with us before or been referred by someone who has.

Transparency, always

You'll always know exactly where your deal stands, what the options are, and what it's going to cost. We don't hide fees, oversell outcomes, or tell you what you want to hear. Just straight, honest advice.

Speed without shortcuts

Fast doesn't mean sloppy. We move quickly because we do the work properly — structuring applications correctly, anticipating lender requirements, and staying on top of every deal so nothing sits in a queue.
